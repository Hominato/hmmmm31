Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer money to another account?

Navigate to the Transfers page from the sidebar menu. Choose your source account, select the destination account or beneficiary, enter the dollar amount, and click Confirm Transfer.

How do I freeze or unfreeze my debit card?

Go to the Cards page. Locate your Everyday Debit Card or Credit Card and toggle the Freeze Card switch. The card status will update immediately to block new purchases.

How do I download a monthly statement PDF?

Visit the Statements page in the navigation bar. You will see a list of monthly statement periods. Click Download PDF or View Summary to print.

How do I deposit a check using mobile deposit?

Click Deposits in the sidebar. Upload sample images of the front and back of your check, enter the deposit amount, and click Submit Deposit.
